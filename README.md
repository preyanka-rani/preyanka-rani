## 🧠 About My Work & Research
I am a results-driven Machine Learning Engineer and Data Science Researcher. I focus on bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and production-grade enterprise software, transforming complex datasets into actionable insights and intelligent autonomous systems.

* 🔬 **Data Science & Research:** I have conducted extensive research developing systems for Bangla fake news detection, sign language detection for speech-impaired individuals, and potato leaf disease classification. I also spearheaded research on Bengali Regional Accent Speech Recognition to build robust, dialect-aware models.
* 🤖 **Agentic AI & LLMs:** I specialize in designing Agentic RAG architectures and automated AI systems utilizing LangChain, LangGraph, and n8n to connect internal company data with live sources. I have deep expertise in integrating models like LLaMA, GPT, Gemini, and Groq via robust FastAPI backends.
* 👁️ **Vision & Speech AI:** I develop and deploy OCR technology solutions using EasyOCR, YOLOv8, and PaddleOCR to process banking documents and extract data. I also build real-time Speech AI systems using Whisper, WebRTC, and Google STT/TTS.
* ⚙️ **MLOps & Engineering:** I establish comprehensive MLOps practices incorporating Docker, Jenkins, and MLflow to streamline the deployment of AI workflows and monitor continuous model experiments.

* **Automated System Fixer & Telegram Issue Tracker:** Built a Python-backed troubleshooting tool that integrates an n8n workflow, allowing users to interact via Telegram to seamlessly create new issues and trigger automated error resolutions.
* **AI-Powered Enterprise Work Log Automation System:** Developed a platform utilizing LangGraph, LangChain, LLaMA 70B, and Google STT that automates employee work log reporting through text and voice interactions via multi-agent orchestration.

* **Knowledge Graph RAG System:** Developed a context-aware RAG system integrating Neo4j knowledge graphs and Hybrid Search to provide precise responses over complex enterprise datasets.
